# Sydney Travel Guide Design System

## Intent

Editorial travel guide for Sydney. Calm, practical, a little cinematic. The design should feel like a premium printed trip dossier translated to the web.

## Palette

- Background: warm paper and mist tones, not pure white.
- Ink: deep blue-black for text.
- Primary accent: harbor teal.
- Secondary accent: sandstone/copper.
- Support colors: muted seafoam, gold, and soft coral for status and emphasis.

## Typography

- Use a strong, readable sans serif stack for body text.
- Use a serif-leaning display stack for major headings when available.
- Keep numbers tabular where practical.
- Favor sentence case and short labels.

## Layout

- Use a centered container with generous breathing room.
- Prefer asymmetrical cards and layered panels over symmetric grids.
- Keep mobile layouts single-column and easy to scan.
- Use one sticky global nav and one bottom/side quick-nav, not many competing nav patterns.

## Components

- Hero: one strong title, one short summary, one compact metadata rail, one action row.
- Sections: paper cards with subtle borders, depth, and clear heading hierarchy.
- Cards: soft elevation, accent rule, and clear hover feedback.
- Tables: readable on desktop, stacked on mobile.
- Pills/badges: use for status only, not as decoration.

## Motion

- Use transform and opacity only.
- Keep transitions short and subtle.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Voice

- Direct and practical.
- Avoid hype words and filler.
- Keep travel notes specific and useful.
